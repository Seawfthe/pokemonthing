VIDEO + POSTER SURVEY  (give this straight after they watch)
============================================================
YCIS  |  IGCSE Global Perspectives  |  anonymous  |  2 minutes
Topic: what did the video and poster actually do?

This is NOT the pre/post survey. That one measures the students.
This one measures your video and poster.


TITLE:  What Did You Think of the Video and Poster?

INTRO
  Anonymous and quick - about 2 minutes. There are no right or wrong
  answers. We want to know if our video and poster worked.
  Please do not write your name.


----------------------------------------------------------
THE 8 QUESTIONS
----------------------------------------------------------

Q1. Did you watch the video?                       [Multiple choice]

    - Yes, all of it
    - Yes, part of it
    - No

Q2. How clear was the video's message?
                                        [Linear scale 1 - 10]
    1 = very unclear        10 = very clear

Q3. How clear was the poster's message?
                                        [Linear scale 1 - 10]
    1 = very unclear        10 = very clear

Q4. Which one made the message easier to understand?
                                                   [Multiple choice]

    - The video
    - The poster
    - Both about the same
    - Neither

Q5. How much did it make you think about your own game spending?
                                        [Linear scale 1 - 10]
    1 = not at all          10 = a lot

Q6. After seeing it, how likely are you to spend less on games?
                                        [Linear scale 1 - 10]
    1 = not likely          10 = very likely

Q7. What is one thing you remember from the video or poster?
    (one line, no right answer)                       [Short answer]

Q8. Overall, how useful were the video and poster together?
                                        [Linear scale 1 - 10]
    1 = not useful          10 = very useful


----------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO READ THE RESULTS
----------------------------------------------------------

  Q2, Q3, Q8   ->  compare the averages. If Q2 is well above Q3, the
                   video did the work and the poster needs changing.
  Q4           ->  the percentage split tells you which format
                   students actually read.
  Q5, Q6       ->  these are your "impact" numbers. Q6 is the one to
                   quote in your report.
  Q7           ->  group the answers into 2-3 themes and count them.

  Example sentence:
  "Students rated the video's clarity at 8.1 out of 10 and the poster
  at 6.3. After seeing both, students rated their likelihood of
  spending less on games at 6.8 out of 10."


----------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT FITS WITH THE OTHER SURVEY
----------------------------------------------------------

  1. Pre survey        ->  before anything
  2. Show video + poster
  3. Post survey       ->  the same 8 questions again (measures change)
  4. This survey       ->  right after, measures the video and poster

  If you only have time for one, run the pre/post pair. This survey is
  the extra one that tells you WHY something did or did not change.
