SURVEY - PRE AND POST (same 8 questions both times)
====================================================
YCIS  |  IGCSE Global Perspectives  |  anonymous  |  2 minutes
Topic: spending on games / online games

Use the SAME questions before and after, so the answers can be compared.
Only the title and the intro line change.

PRE  title: How Do You Spend on Games?
POST title: How Do You Spend on Games? (After)


INTRO - PRE
  Anonymous and quick - about 2 minutes. There are no right or wrong
  answers. Answer honestly, and do not write your name.

INTRO - POST
  Same questions as before, about 2 minutes. There are no right or
  wrong answers. Answer honestly, and do not write your name.


----------------------------------------------------------
THE 8 QUESTIONS
----------------------------------------------------------

Q1. How much money do you get in a normal month?
    (allowance, red packets, part-time work)      [Multiple choice]

    - Under HK$200
    - HK$200 - 499
    - HK$500 - 999
    - HK$1,000 - 1,999
    - HK$2,000 or more

Q2. About what percentage of that goes on games?  [Multiple choice]

    - 0 - 10%
    - 11 - 25%
    - 26 - 50%
    - 51 - 75%
    - 76 - 100%

Q3. What do you spend the most on in games?
                                                  [Multiple choice]

    - Skins and cosmetic items
    - Gacha pulls and loot boxes
    - New games
    - Battle passes and subscriptions
    - Something else

Q4. How often do you spend money on games?
                                                  [Multiple choice]

    - Never
    - 1 - 2 times a month
    - 3 - 5 times a month
    - 6 or more times a month

Q5. How much do you enjoy spending on games?
                                        [Linear scale 1 - 10]
    1 = not at all          10 = a lot

Q6. How much do you plan your game spending before you buy?
                                        [Linear scale 1 - 10]
    1 = never plan          10 = always plan

Q7. How often do you spend more on games than you meant to?
                                        [Linear scale 1 - 10]
    1 = never               10 = very often

Q8. After buying something in a game, how do you usually feel?
                                        [Linear scale 1 - 10]
    1 = wish I had not      10 = glad I did


----------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO READ THE RESULTS
----------------------------------------------------------

  Q1 to Q4  ->  report the percentage who picked each option.
  Q5 to Q8  ->  work out the average (mean) score.
  Before vs after  ->  compare the averages for Q5 to Q8. That is your
                       "did anything change" number.

  Example sentence:
  "The average for 'how often do you spend more on games than you
  meant to' fell from 6.4 before the session to 5.1 afterwards."
