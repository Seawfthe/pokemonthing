Section description: Circle the letter of the answer you think is
best. "Not sure" is a real option.
----------------------------------------------------------

1. Which of these is the best definition of "overspending"?
                                                          [Multiple choice]
   A. Spending money on anything that is fun
   B. Spending more than you planned, or more than your income allows
   C. Buying something you do not need
   D. Spending money every single day
   E. Not sure

2. You have HK$300 for the month. You spend all of it on a game skin
   and skip the school trip you wanted to go on. The opportunity cost
   of the skin is:                                        [Multiple choice]
   A. The HK$300 you paid for the skin
   B. The school trip you gave up
   C. The skin's resale value
   D. Nothing, because you paid the correct price
   E. Not sure

3. A "gacha" or "loot box" system gives players a random item after
   they pay. Its main business purpose is to:            [Multiple choice]
   A. Make the game fairer for every player
   B. Encourage repeated spending because the reward is uncertain
   C. Let players earn real money back from the game
   D. Help players finish the game faster
   E. Not sure

4. A game shows the message "LIMITED BANNER - ENDS IN 6 HOURS".
   This is designed mainly to use:                       [Multiple choice]
   A. Loss aversion and FOMO (fear of missing out)
   B. The fact that the item really does get cheaper later
   C. The player's need to sleep
   D. Poor internet connection
   E. Not sure

5. True or false: "I have already put HK$800 into this game, so I
   should keep playing so that the money is not wasted."
                                                          [Multiple choice]
   A. True
   B. False
   C. Not sure

6. A shop label reads "WAS HK$1,299 - NOW HK$699". This is an example
   of:                                                   [Multiple choice]
   A. Anchoring - the higher first price makes HK$699 feel cheap
   B. Inflation
   C. Opportunity cost
   D. Brand loyalty
   E. Not sure

7. A survey finds that students who spend more on games also sleep
   less. Which conclusion is best supported by the data?
                                                          [Multiple choice]
   A. Spending on games causes poor sleep
   B. Poor sleep causes students to spend more
   C. The two are associated, but the data alone cannot show which
      causes which
   D. The survey must be wrong
   E. Not sure

8. You receive HK$2,000 in a month and save HK$400 of it. Your
   savings rate is:                                      [Multiple choice]
   A. 20%
   B. 25%
   C. 5%
   D. 40%
   E. Not sure

9. Which of these is NOT usually a warning sign of overspending?
                                                          [Multiple choice]
   A. Borrowing money to pay for entertainment
   B. Hiding purchases from your family
   C. Planning a purchase in advance and saving up for it
   D. Feeling regret most times you buy something
   E. Not sure

10. In Hong Kong, if a student under 18 spends money on in-game
    top-ups and later regrets it, which statement is most accurate?
                                                          [Multiple choice]
   A. The game company must always refund any amount within 7 days
   B. A refund is not guaranteed; it depends on the company's policy
      and the circumstances
   C. Minors are not legally allowed to buy anything online
   D. The Consumer Council can order the company to refund
   E. Not sure
